About the Property

Experience the pinnacle of coastal luxury at The Bliss Oceanfront Hotel. Nestled directly on the pristine sands of My Khe Beach, our property offers panoramic views of the East Vietnam Sea and the iconic Son Tra Peninsula. Each room is meticulously designed with contemporary Vietnamese elegance, featuring floor-to-ceiling windows that invite the ocean breeze and natural light.

Whether you're lounging by our rooftop infinity pool, indulging in world-class spa treatments, or savoring fresh local seafood at our signature restaurant, The Bliss provides an unforgettable sanctuary in the heart of Da Nang.
